# Independent metamorphic audit argument

The audit is evidence about the frozen finite model, not a proof of executable
service equivalence.  `frontier_a` enumerates exactly the closed O/B/N
configurations and performs reverse reachability from all-new.
`schedulable_b` separately explores forward schedules with a dictionary-based
closure predicate.  Equality on all 2^14 two-service assignments therefore
checks the decision implementation over that complete domain.  The additional
closure, reachability, repair-monotonicity, renaming, path-length, and
witness-deletion checks are independent necessary consequences of the model.
The larger seeded topologies and twelve-role scale families broaden execution
coverage but do not establish correctness beyond the declared finite rules.
